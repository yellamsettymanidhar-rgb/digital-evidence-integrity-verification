How this works

The system computes a fresh SHA-256 hash of the file you upload here and compares it, character by character, against the hash recorded when the evidence was originally registered.

Because of SHA-256's avalanche effect, changing even a single byte anywhere in the file — even something as small as re-saving it in the same format — produces a completely different hash. A match means the file is byte-for-byte identical to what was registered.

This confirms integrity (no change since registration), not absolute authenticity or origin.
